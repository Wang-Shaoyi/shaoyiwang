Shaoyi Wang

Shaoyi Wang is a first-year Ph.D. student in Architecture at the College of Environmental Design, UC Berkeley, under the supervision of Prof. Simon Schleicher and Prof. Ramon Weber. Her research focuses on computational design and automated design pipelines, with the goal of exploring intelligent and scalable workflows for architecture, structures, and fabrication. She is also interested in developing design tools, interactive applications, and games as extensions of computational thinking in design. Before starting her Ph.D., Shaoyi completed her M.S. in Architecture at UC Berkeley and received her B.Arch. from Tsinghua University in 2023, with a minor in Psychology.
