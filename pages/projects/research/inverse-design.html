An ongoing research project exploring inverse design for architectural systems.

Inverse design Computational design

Overview

This project investigates how desired design outcomes can be used as inputs for generating and evaluating architectural solutions.

Research focus

The study focuses on computational representations, design-space exploration, and the relationship between performance objectives and design decisions.

Status

This research is currently in progress. Additional methods, experiments, and results will be added as the project develops.
